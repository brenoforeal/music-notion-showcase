Scoring rules

Albums: Production 15% · Individual 20% · Creativity 25% · Experience 40%, 0 to 10. EPs and compilations: Experience 60% · Individual 40%. Other songs contribute their Individual Rating only. Scores are stored raw; the calculator rounds what it shows to the nearest 0.1, keeping exact .x5 (9.485 → 9.5, 9.33 → 9.3, 9.25 → 9.25). Ties: Experience → Creativity → Individual → Production (EPs: Experience → Individual).
